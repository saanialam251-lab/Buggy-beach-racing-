# 3D animation upgrade

The scene now includes a stronger 3D presentation rather than a static 2D-style preview:

- Soft shadows and high-performance WebGL renderer settings.
- Dynamic chase, cockpit, top, and wide camera movement.
- Animated suspension/body bounce and steering roll.
- Jump arc animation with landing movement.
- Multi-layer animated nitro flames.
- Boost speed lines with depth movement and FOV expansion.
- Animated dust particles during jumps and boosts.
- Moving volumetric-style cloud puffs.
- Day, sunset, and night lighting presets.
- Ocean/beach/building environment remains enabled.

This uses original procedural geometry. For production-quality photorealism, add optimized licensed GLB assets and texture maps.
